# 1\. **Prompt Guidelines**

## 1\.1 **Prompt Structure**

|- To increase model's adherence generate non\-copyright content :<br>Add  in the prompt "Ensure all music, visuals, characters, logos, brands, and video elements are fully original, generic, copyright\-safe, and free from any identifiable copyrighted or legally protected references\." <br><br>- For scenarios where BGM is not required,<br>Add in the prompt  *"Do not generate background music"* to the prompt\. <br>This preserves human dialogue and sound effects while preventing the system from auto\-generating background music, enabling more flexible audio layering\.|
